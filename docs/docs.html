The reader

Choose single or double pages, manga order or vertical reading when available. Adjust zoom and fit, save bookmarks and resume your progress. Controls vary by platform.

04

Files and formats

Local reading recognizes CBZ, ZIP, CBR, RAR, PDF, 7Z, CB7, TAR and CBT. Image-based EPUB support is experimental. Some desktop archives require a compatible extraction tool.

05

Offline and account

Guest reading is local and works offline. An account is optional for online services such as synchronization and community features. Backups differ between desktop and Android, so keep a copy for each platform.
